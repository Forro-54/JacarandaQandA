Jacaranda Q&A 01.00.11

- Changes the guest 5-minute correction facility to a single-use correction opportunity.
- A guest may make one correction within five minutes; saving it immediately consumes the guest edit credential and ends further correction access.
- Guest correction now returns to the normal Q&A page with a confirmation that the corrected question is awaiting moderation.
- Guest correction still permits changes only to the question title and question text; public name and private email remain locked.
- Published questions are collapsed by default on a normal first visit so the archive initially shows question titles and statuses rather than every full conversation.
- Selecting a question title expands that conversation; opening another collapses the previously open question.
- Adds accessible aria-expanded state to the question-title controls.
- Administrator Answer mode, Ask a follow-up, completion links and direct question/hash links automatically expand the relevant conversation.
- Retains the Answered + original-questioner rule for Ask a follow-up.
- Retains the one-question-at-a-time / not-a-discussion-forum participation guidance and #F5F5DC eye-relief palette.
- No database schema changes; safe in-place upgrade from 01.00.10.
